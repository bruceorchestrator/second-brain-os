**Produces:** a research brief on a topic, 5-8 post ideas, and post drafts in the owner's voice.
**For:** the owner's X account. The owner edits and posts by hand.
**Status:** manual, on request. A morning routine only after two weeks of daily manual use.

## Read first, in order
1. `CONTEXT.md`: modes and gates.
2. `_config/rules.md`: fact checking, formats, writing rules, red list.
3. `_config/voice.md` before writing anything in the owner's name. **No real samples in it: stop and ask for them. Do not draft.**
4. Only the stage you run: `stages/NN_*/CONTEXT.md`.

## Never
- Post, reply, like, follow or DM. Nothing in this folder touches the account.
- Invent numbers, quotes, events or the owner's experience. Unverified stays marked unverified.
- Present a commentator's retelling as the original source's words.

## Memory contract
Briefs, ideas and drafts go to `stages/*/output/`. One line per run to `log.md` and to
`memory/daily/<today>.md`. A finding that matters beyond X (a market fact, a tool, a person)
also goes to `memory/wiki/`.

# Rules

## Fact checking
- Every number, quote and event is checked against its primary source before it reaches the owner: the original post, the official statement, the filing, the transcript. Its link goes into the fact table under the draft.
- Unverified stays visibly unverified. Never smooth it over.
- A link handed over "with video" or "with photos" is opened first to confirm the media is there.
- Quote what the source said, not what someone retold.

## Formats
- Short post: one idea, under 280 characters, the first line carries it.
- Long post: a hook line, 3-6 short paragraphs, one concrete example or number, no hashtags.
- Thread: only when every post stands on its own; 3-7 posts.
- FILL IN: what the owner actually likes, from the verdicts in `log.md`.

## Writing
- `voice.md` wins over every rule here.
- No stock AI phrasing: "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in", "game-changer", "unlock", lists of three for rhythm, a question at the end only to farm replies.
- No invented personal stories. The owner's experience comes from the owner, `memory/daily/` or `memory/wiki/`.

## Red list
- FILL IN: topics the owner never posts about.
- No engagement bait ("like if you agree"), no fake urgency, no claims the owner cannot back.

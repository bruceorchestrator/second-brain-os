# Stage 02 - Ideas (L2)

## Inputs
- `stages/01_research/output/<the latest brief, or the one the owner names>`. Optional in Ideas mode.
- `memory/USER.md`: who the owner is, their audience, what they can speak to from experience.
- `memory/STATE.md` and the last 7 days of `memory/daily/`: what the owner is doing now (own-experience ideas).
- `log.md`: ideas killed or posted in the last 30 days. Do not repeat them.
- `_config/rules.md`: formats and red list.

## Process
1. Write 5-8 ideas. Mix them: news with the owner's angle, the owner's own experience, a contrarian take, a how-to.
2. Each idea: the hook line, what it rests on (a brief finding or the owner's experience), why the owner can say it and not just anyone, the format.
3. Rank them and recommend the top 3, one reason each.
4. Nothing from the red list. An idea that needs an unverified fact says so.

## Outputs
`output/YYYY-MM-DD_ideas.md`:
```
# Ideas (YYYY-MM-DD)
Recommended: 2, 5, 1 (one reason each)

| # | Hook | Rests on | Why the owner | Format | keep / kill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ... | brief finding 3 | ... | long | |
```

## Gate
Stop. Give the owner the file path and the recommended three. They write `keep` or `kill` in the last column, or edit a hook. Stage 03 drafts only the `keep` rows of the edited file.

## Done when
- 5-8 ideas, each tied to a finding or to the owner's own experience, top 3 recommended, and the agent is waiting.

# Stage 03 - Drafts (L2)

## Inputs
- The `keep` rows of `stages/02_ideas/output/<date>_ideas.md`, or the owner's own notes (Draft and Own story modes).
- `_config/voice.md`: **must hold real samples.** If it does not, stop and ask the owner for them.
- `_config/rules.md`: formats, fact checking, writing rules.
- The brief each idea rests on, for its facts and links.

## Process
1. Draft each idea in its format. From the owner's own notes: keep their phrasing, change only what the format needs.
2. Under each draft, a fact table: every number, name and claim, its source link, verified or not.
3. Read the draft against `voice.md` (does it sound like the samples?) and the writing rules. Rewrite what fails.
4. If the post needs media, name it and where it comes from; open the link to confirm it is there.

## Outputs
`output/YYYY-MM-DD_drafts.md`:
```
## 1. <first line of the post>
<the post>

Format: long | Media: none / <link, checked>
| Claim | Source | Verified |
|---|---|---|
```

## Gate
The owner edits and posts by hand. The agent never posts.

## Done when
- Every draft has a complete fact table, and nothing unverified is presented as fact.
- `log.md` has a line for the run. After the owner's verdict, the line gets what they changed.
